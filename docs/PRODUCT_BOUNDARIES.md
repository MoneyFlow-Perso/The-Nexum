# Product boundaries

## The Nexum
The Nexum is a Social Media Branding & Growth Hub for fashion brands.

It owns:
- Brand Intelligence and consistency rules
- Creative Director discussion mode
- Quick Campaign mode and garment analysis
- Visual, banner, editorial and virtual try-on prompt workflows
- Short-form video and reel blueprints
- Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Pinterest copy/SEO
- Paid-ad concepts, A/B variants, shop-the-look and DM closer scripts
- Campaign planning, review and asset library

## UmBrage
UmBrage remains the operational business system. It owns:
- Customer messaging and communication
- Sales and order taking
- Order processing and delivery coordination
- CRM/customer records
- Financial records and operational business data

## Integration decision
Level 2 is the initial target:
- The Nexum may read only selected, approved, allowlisted UmBrage information.
- Initial integration must be read-only.
- The Nexum must remain useful if UmBrage is unavailable.
- No browser-side service-role keys, database passwords, access tokens or other
  privileged credentials.
- No direct modification of UmBrage records or operational workflows.

Level 3 is a future option only:
- Selected marketing insights or write-back may be considered later.
- It requires a separate dependency review, permission design, approval and
  regression plan.

## Non-negotiable rule
The Nexum creates demand. UmBrage manages the business generated by that demand.
